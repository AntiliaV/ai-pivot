### 2026-11-12 — Pacing mappings: common scoring method adopted

Adopted one method for all intervention scores: each intervention scored once against a common yardstick (a coordinated cap on frontier training compute, the Agenda's Case A), on anchored 1–5 scales, with invasiveness for labs (1–5) and readiness added. Scores shown to policymakers as meters with words, not raw numbers. *Why:* to make different tools comparable and the scores defensible. Touched: new `4. Drafting/Scoring method.md`; the scores tables in the three mappings.

### 2026-11-12 — Pacing mappings: incentive compatibility kept in words, not scored

Decided not to give incentive compatibility a 1–5 score. It stays verbal (cooperate, circumvent or undermine), stated for each of four actors: labs, chip and cloud firms, the leading state, the trailing state. *Why:* the same intervention draws opposite responses from different actors, so one number would mislead (reason to confirm). Touched: the scores tables in the three background mappings in `3. Own projects/Pacing — verification vs invasiveness/4. Drafting/`.
