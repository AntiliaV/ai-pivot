# AI Pivot: moving into AI safety work after the DPhil

**Start here.** This folder holds everything for Antilia's move into AI safety work after her DPhil in Economics at Oxford (scheduled to finish in October 2027). How the folder is maintained, and how Claude works on it, is in `CLAUDE.md`.

## The goal

Choose between two paths, **AI safety grantmaking** and **EU AI policy and governance** (with **AI economics research** and **model evaluation** kept open for comparison). Build a record that shows fit, through courses, programmes, networking and her own projects. Then apply.

The two own projects are the **pacing project** (with Ernest Lo and Charles Dillon: the trade-offs between verification and invasiveness for mechanisms that verify, monitor or enforce the pacing of frontier-AI development), which she works on at least one day a week, and the **AI character evaluation project** (measuring altruism in AI agents with experimental-economics designs).
